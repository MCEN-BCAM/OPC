# Data terms

The experimental contributors authorised public inclusion of the data in this repository for the associated OPC modelling study.

This statement records permission to distribute the files; it does not by itself assign a standard open-data licence or waive contributor rights. Before creating a formal archived release, the authors should select and state the intended reuse terms (for example, CC BY 4.0 or CC0) in consultation with the contributing institutions.

Until then, users should cite the associated article and contact the study authors regarding reuse beyond verification and reproduction of the published analysis.
